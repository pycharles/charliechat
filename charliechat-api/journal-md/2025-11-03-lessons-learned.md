# Lessons from Building Charlie Chat — and What’s Next

When I started Charlie Chat, my goal was to learn, experiment, and show how AI could make a resume interactive. The response exceeded what I expected — and the process itself taught me a lot about communication, focus, and shipping something end to end.

That project helped me clarify what kind of work energizes me most, and I’m excited to now bring that same mindset into my new role as Director of Security at JumpCloud.

For anyone working on personal projects during a career transition: build something that stretches you and reflects how you think. Even if it’s small, you’ll come out sharper and with a clearer story to tell.
